# Representations and Cohomology I

D. J. Benson. *Representations and Cohomology I: Basic representation theory
of finite groups and associative algebras.* Cambridge Studies in Advanced
Mathematics 30. Cambridge University Press, second edition, 1998 (first
published 1991).

The book develops the representation theory of finite groups and
finite dimensional algebras with a homological flavour: rings and modules,
homological algebra, modules for group algebras, Auslander–Reiten theory
and the representations of quivers, representation rings and Burnside
rings, and block theory, including blocks with cyclic and Klein four defect
groups.

This edition includes the author's later comments and corrections to the
1998 edition. The [releases](https://github.com/stdcall/benson-1/releases)
provide the book with editorial notes, a separate list of corrections and
SHA-256 checksums.
